Introduction 
The software engineering Final Year Project Biz-ight aims to create a decision support and 
automation dashboard which will support micro and home-based businesses. The project 
helps small business owners to control their sales operations and inventory management and 
pricing strategies and expense tracking while enabling them to make decisions based on data 
analysis. 
 The project requires students to create an innovative software system that solves real-world 
problems which micro-entrepreneurs face when they operate their businesses through social 
media platforms and basic e-commerce websites. 
 These businesses often rely on manual record-keeping and intuition-based decisions, which 
leads to inefficiencies and financial losses. Biz-ight combines operational e-commerce 
system data with its decision support system and automation processes and AI-based 
explanations to generate operational insights. 
Objective 
The main goals of this project are: 
• To create a web-based decision support dashboard which will assist micro-businesses 
in their business operations  
• To achieve automatic sales and inventory and expense monitoring through system 
integration  
• To deliver rule-based business insights which will guide pricing and inventory 
control and expense tracking  
• To enable decision-making through AI-based summary and explanation systems 
• To test system performance in decreasing manual tasks while enhancing business 
decision-making processes. 
Background/Existing System 
Micro-businesses depend on social media networks and basic e-commerce platforms to 
operate their sales activities. The platforms enable users to perform transactions but they 
mainly concentrate on processing orders and maintaining records instead of assisting with 
business analysis and decision-making tasks. 
 Businesses encounter difficulties because existing inventory management and accounting 
software solutions:  
• Present excessive technical difficulties which non-technical users cannot handle 
• Targeting enterprise customers  
• Provide static reports which do not deliver operational recommendations 
 Business owners encounter difficulties because they need to deal with: 
• Unreliable methods for establishing product prices 
• Insufficient methods for managing their stock. Their operational costs which they 
must manage remain hidden 
• They make decisions based on immediate requirements instead of planning ahead  
 
 
Small business owners show increasing interest in simplified automated intelligent systems 
which deliver business functions dedicated to their needs according to market patterns.  
The available solutions fail to support decision-making processes because they either use 
non-transparent artificial intelligence systems or they do not present accessible decision
making solutions to small business operations. The integrated platform unites rule-based 
decision support with workflow automation and AI-based explanation capabilities to solve 
existing system deficiencies. 
 
Proposed Solution 
The proposed solution is a decision support and automation system integrated with an e
commerce website. The system collects sales and inventory data through APIs or webhooks 
and processes it using predefined business rules. 
The solution follows a layered approach: 
 
Data Integration Layer 
• Receives sales events from the e-commerce system 
• Updates inventory and transaction records 
Decision Support Layer 
These KPIs form the basis of rule-based recommendations. 
Key Performance Indicators: 
 
• Revenue Growth Rate (RGR) 
RGR = (Revenue_current − Revenue_previous) / Revenue_previous 
• Inventory Turnover Ratio (ITR) 
ITR = Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory 
• Expense Ratio (ER) 
ER = Total Expenses / Total Revenue 
• Stock Coverage Period (SCP) 
SCP = Current Stock / Daily Sales Rate 
 
Rule-Based Evaluation Examples 
The system applies deterministic rule conditions such as: 
• If Current Stock < Reorder Threshold → Low Stock Alert 
• If Stock Coverage Period < 3 days → Urgent Reorder Recommendation 
• If Inventory Turnover Ratio < 1.5 → Slow-Moving Inventory Warning 
• If Expense Ratio > 0.6 → Expense Spike Warning 
• If Revenue Growth Rate < -0.1 → Sales Decline Alert 
Threshold values are configurable and may be adjusted based on business category. 
 
Business Health Score 
The overall business performance is aggregated into a Business Health Score (BHS): 
 
 𝐻𝑆=0.4(𝐼𝑇𝑅𝑛𝑜𝑟𝑚)+0.35(𝑅𝐺𝑅𝑛𝑜𝑟𝑚)+0.25(1−𝐸𝑅𝑛𝑜𝑟𝑚) 
 
 
 
All KPI values are normalized between 0 and 1 before aggregation to ensure balanced 
contribution to the final score. 
 
AI-Assisted Explanation Layer: 
 
The AI-Assisted Explanation Layer does not make business decisions. The rule-based 
Decision Support Engine generates all decision outcomes. This layer exists to transform 
structured system outputs into understandable explanations that non-technical business users 
can comprehend.  
The AI module receives predefined KPI values and triggered rule outputs from the decision 
engine. Structured prompt templates are used to develop explanations that match system
calculated metrics while avoiding any external assumptions.
